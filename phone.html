Before you drive

  • Lay the phone in the Tesla phone dock, the angled tray with the wireless charger, any way round. It charges there while it streams. A dash or vent holder works too.
  • Keep this page open with the screen on. If the phone sleeps, the car carries on with GPS.
  • Calibration is automatic: just drive off normally.

The sound plays from the car. This phone only sends motion: no audio, no location.
